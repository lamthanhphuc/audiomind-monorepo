# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AudioMind is used by people reviewing recorded or realtime meetings: product teams, engineering teams, business analysts, and operators who need transcripts, searchable evidence, AI summaries, and exportable action plans without losing source context.

## Product Purpose

AudioMind captures meeting audio, turns it into transcript evidence, and produces AI-assisted meeting analysis. Success means a reviewer can find what was said, understand decisions and follow-up work, verify evidence, and export a useful handoff document.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The product should feel like a dependable workbench for analysis rather than a marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, oversized hero patterns, raw JSON dumps, vague AI claims without evidence, and interfaces that hide provenance for transcript-backed conclusions.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before flourish: analysis and action items should stay tied to transcript context.
- Operational clarity: status, errors, empty states, and exports should be explicit and recoverable.
- Dense but calm: support repeated review work with scannable hierarchy and restrained visual emphasis.
- Preserve trust boundaries: never expose secrets, raw provider payloads, or unverifiable model evidence in UI or logs.
- Local-first defaults: respect the repo's Deepgram and Gemini MVP runtime without implying local Whisper or Ollama are active.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus states, and readable Vietnamese/English mixed content. Avoid motion that is required for comprehension and keep controls usable for long review sessions.
